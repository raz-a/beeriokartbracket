# 4th Annual Northwestern Beerio Kart Invitational Rules

## Race Types and Rules

There are two types of races that racers will participate in:

- **Vanilla Mario Kart**
- **Beerio Kart**

Both Vanilla Mario Kart and Beerio Kart will be played with the following configurations:

- 150cc
- VS. Item Box set to Recommended
- VS. Laps set to Recommended
- Racers can use any character/kart combination.
- Course selection is always random.

New to this year is the introduction of **8-player races**, with the following points awarded for each placement:

| Placement | Points |
| :-------: | :----: |
|    1st    | **8**  |
|    2nd    | **7**  |
|    3rd    | **6**  |
|    4th    | **5**  |
|    5th    | **4**  |
|    6th    | **3**  |
|    7th    | **2**  |
|    8th    | **1**  |

### Beerio Kart adds the following rules to the race

**1. Racers must complete 1 drink in the span of the race.**

> *A drink must be a 12 oz. canned beverage (alcoholic or non-alcoholic). Some examples include (but are not limited to):*
>
> - A 12 oz. can of beer
> - A 12 oz. can of hard seltzer
> - A 12 oz. can of seltzer/sparkling water
>
> If you have any questions about the beverage you'd like to consume during the race, reach out to a tournament official for a ruling.
>
> ***If you complete the race before you complete your drink, you will automatically receive an '8th Place' placement and receive 1 point.***
>
> *Addendum: At the end of a race, if a drink has a few drops left, it is acceptable (WITHIN REASON). If there is a dispute about a drink being empty at the end of the race, a tournament official will have the final call.*

**2. Racers *cannot* drink and drive at the same time.**

> - A racer can only have either the controller or their beverage in their hand at one time.
> - In front of each racer's chair will be a marked square known as the **beer zone**.
> - When drinking, **the racer's controller must be in the beer zone**.
> - When racing, **the racer's drink must be in the beer zone**.
>
> There is no requirement to park your vehicle on the side of the road.

## Race Scheduling

The 2026 Beerio Kart Tournament is separated into three parts, each with slightly different formats and rules.

### 1. Pools

All players will participate in pools to qualify for brackets and to set seeds.

Players will be bucketed based on the number of races they have already completed. There are 9 buckets, representing 0 completed races all the way up to 8 completed races.

After registration, all players will begin in the "0 races" bucket.

Players will be randomly chosen from the lowest bucket to participate in a single race (depending on the number of registered participants, this will be a 6-player, 7-player, or 8-player race). After the race, points will be awarded according to each player's placement and added to their total score. The players will then be moved to the next bucket.

Each even bucket is played using the Beerio Kart ruleset, while odd buckets use the Vanilla ruleset.

Pools races will continue until all players are in the "8 races" bucket.

At the end of the pools races, the players with the **16** highest scores will advance to the bracket. Any ties will be resolved with an additional race using the Vanilla ruleset.

### 2. Bracket (16 racers)

The top 16 players from pools will participate in a **double elimination** bracket. Each heat of the bracket will consist of **3 races**:

- The 1st and 3rd races will use the Vanilla ruleset.
- The 2nd race will use the Beerio Kart ruleset.

The racers with the **Top 4** total scores will move on to the next round, while the remaining racers will either be placed in the losers' bracket (if this is a winners' bracket match) or be eliminated (if this is a losers' bracket match).

> If there is a tie after the three races, a 4th race will be held using the Vanilla ruleset.

Brackets will continue until there are 4 players remaining in both the winners' and losers' brackets.

### 3. The Grand Finals Gauntlet

Each racer will be given a set of 'lives':

- Racers from the winners' bracket will receive **6** lives.
- Racers from the losers' bracket will receive **3** lives.

Racers will participate in back-to-back races. Racers that place in the **bottom** half of all racers (rounding up) will lose 1 life.

If a racer runs out of lives, they are eliminated. Once 4 players remain, races will move to a single screen.

Races will continue until one racer remains.

- Every 3rd race will use the Beerio Kart ruleset.
